roma-vps-backup

A personal backup setup for its owner. It uses rclone to save locally encrypted snapshots of the owner's AI Council discussion data from a private server to the owner's Google Drive. It is not offered to other users.

Google Drive access is limited to files created by this app. Backup content and filenames are encrypted on the owner's server before upload. The owner controls the server, Google account, and encryption keys.
